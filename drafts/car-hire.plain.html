Before you pick up your car

  • Bring a valid licence. The main driver needs a full licence held for at least one year, plus a credit card in their own name.
  • Check the age rules. Drivers under 25 may pay a young-driver surcharge at the counter.
  • Plan one-way trips. Returning the car in another city is possible on many routes. Some suppliers add a one-way charge that you pay locally.
  • Winter driving. From December to March, cars rented in Québec come with winter tires.

Car rental questions

Can I return the car to a different location?

Yes. Pick a different drop-off location in the search form. If the supplier charges a one-way fee, we show it before you continue.

What is included in the price?

Every rate includes third-party liability, unlimited kilometres, taxes and airport fees. You can add collision cover and extras on the next steps.

When do I pay?

You pay the total shown at checkout. Fuel, deposits and any one-way charge are handled by the supplier at pick-up.
