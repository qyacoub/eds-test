E-bike rental

Explore the Old Port, the Lachine Canal and the islands on a pedal-assist city e-bike. No experience needed: the motor helps on every hill, and our team shows you the bike before you leave.

The bike

Our e-bikes have a 500 Wh battery with up to 80 km of range, seven gears, hydraulic disc brakes and built-in front and rear lights. A step-through frame makes them easy to get on, and the seat adjusts for riders from 150 to 195 cm.

What's included

  • Helmet in your size, adjusted at pickup.
  • Lock with a key, so you can stop at the markets on the way.
  • Phone mount on the handlebar for maps.
  • Route map of the Lachine Canal path and the Old Port loop.

Taxes included. A $200 deposit is held on a credit card in the renter's name and released when the bike is returned.

Pickup and return

Pick up and return your e-bike at our shop on the King-Edward Pier. Bring photo ID. Riders must be 16 or older, and riders under 18 need a parent's signature. Late returns are charged by the hour.
