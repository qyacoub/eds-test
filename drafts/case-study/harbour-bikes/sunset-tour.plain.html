Old Port sunset ride

A two-hour guided e-bike ride along the St. Lawrence and the Lachine Canal, timed for sunset. Your guide stops at the best viewpoints and shares the stories behind the Old Port, the grain elevators and the canal locks.

The ride

  • Duration: 2 hours, about 14 km at an easy pace.
  • Group size: up to 12 riders with one guide.
  • Included: e-bike, helmet, lights and a hot drink at the Atwater Market stop.
  • Meeting point: our shop on the King-Edward Pier, 15 minutes before departure.

Availability and booking

Rides run on Saturdays all year, weather permitting. The calendar below shows the next dates, the seats left and the price.

Good to know

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure. Riders must be 14 or older. In winter, we provide studded tires and the ride is shortened in very cold weather.
