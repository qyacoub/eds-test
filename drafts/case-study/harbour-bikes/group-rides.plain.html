Group rides and team outings

Bring your team, your class or your family reunion to the Old Port. We prepare the bikes, plan a route for your group's level and send a guide.

Group discounts

  • 8 to 14 riders: 15% off bike rentals and guided rides.
  • 15 to 30 riders: 20% off, plus a second guide.
  • More than 30 riders: contact us for a custom quote.

How to book

Book at least 72 hours before your ride, so we can prepare the bikes and a guide. Groups of 10 or more always ride with a dedicated guide.

Email groups@harbourbikes.example with your date, the number of riders and the ride you'd like. We reply within one business day.
