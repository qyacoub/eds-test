Harbour Bikes

Bike and e-bike rentals and guided rides in Montréal's Old Port, since 2014.

What we offer

  • E-bike rental: pedal-assist city e-bikes by the half day, the day or the weekend.
  • Old Port sunset ride: a two-hour guided ride along the river and the Lachine Canal.
  • Group rides: team outings, classes and family groups from 8 to 30 riders.

Visit us

Our shop is on the King-Edward Pier in the Old Port. Open every day from 9 am to 6 pm from April to November, and on weekends in winter.
