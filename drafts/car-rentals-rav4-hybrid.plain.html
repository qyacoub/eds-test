About this vehicle

The RAV4 Hybrid is our most requested SUV for winter trips out of Montréal. Its all-wheel drive sends power to the rear wheels as soon as the front ones slip, which makes a real difference on snowy hills in the Laurentians or on the road to Mont-Tremblant. The hybrid system is quiet in city traffic and uses about 6 litres per 100 km on the highway, so a full tank comfortably covers a return trip to Québec City.

Inside, five adults fit without squeezing, and the cargo area takes two large suitcases plus skis or snowboards with the rear seats split. Heated front seats, a heated steering wheel and a rear-window defroster come standard. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto connect through the central screen, and two USB-C ports sit in each row.

Pickup and return

Our counter is on the ground floor of the multi-level parking, across from the domestic arrivals exit. Follow the signs for rental cars, then take the walkway to level 1. Bring a valid driver's licence held for at least one year and a credit card in the driver's name; debit cards are not accepted for the security deposit. Drivers must be 21 or older, and a young-driver fee applies under 25.

Return the car with the same fuel level to avoid a refuelling charge. After hours, park on level 1 in a marked bay and drop the key in the secure box next to the counter. An agent checks the car the next morning and emails your final receipt.
