Winter driving in Québec

Renting a car between December and March? Here is what changes in winter, what we include and what it costs.

Winter tires are mandatory

In Québec, every passenger vehicle must have winter tires from December 1 to March 15. All our cars rented in that period leave with certified winter tires.

Winter package price

The winter package costs CAD 12.40 per day, capped at CAD 86.80 per rental. It includes winter tires, a snow brush and ice scraper, and winter windshield washer fluid.

Tips for the road

  • Clear all the snow. Brush the roof, lights and windows before you leave; it's required by law.
  • Plan extra time. Allow 20 to 30 minutes more for airport returns after a snowfall.
  • Check Québec 511. Road conditions and closures are posted on the Québec 511 site.
